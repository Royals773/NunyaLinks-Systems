## Background

The Client wishes to engage NunyaLink to provide the services described in Schedule 1, and NunyaLink agrees to provide them on the terms of this agreement.

## 1. Definitions

- **Deliverables** — the systems, automations, websites, software, documents and other materials NunyaLink creates for the Client under this agreement, as described in Schedule 1.
- **Services** — the work described in Schedule 1, including any agreed changes.
- **Specification** — the written description of the Deliverables approved by the Client at the end of the discovery stage.
- **Working day** — Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in England.

## 2. The Services

2.1 NunyaLink will provide the Services with reasonable skill and care and in line with good industry practice.

2.2 Before building, NunyaLink will agree a Specification with the Client. Once approved, the Specification forms part of Schedule 1.

2.3 Any change to the Services or the Specification must be agreed in writing (email is enough). NunyaLink will tell the Client of any effect on fees or timings before carrying out the change.

## 3. The Client's responsibilities

3.1 The Client will:

- provide information, decisions, content and feedback promptly and make sure it is accurate;
- give NunyaLink access to the systems, accounts and people reasonably needed to deliver the Services;
- make sure it has the right to use, and to let NunyaLink use, any content, data and third-party accounts it provides; and
- name one person with authority to make decisions on the Client's behalf.

3.2 If the Client's delay or failure to do these things holds up the Services, NunyaLink may adjust the timeline and charge for reasonable extra time at its standard rates, after telling the Client.

## 4. Fees and payment

4.1 The fees are set out in Schedule 1. The total fee for the agreed scope is **{{quote.total}}**.

4.2 Payment terms: {{business.payment_terms}}

4.3 Unless stated otherwise, fees do not include VAT, which will be added where applicable, or third-party costs such as software subscriptions, licences, phone numbers or AI usage. These are paid by the Client directly or recharged at cost with the Client's prior agreement.

4.4 If an invoice is not paid when due, NunyaLink may pause work after giving 7 days' written notice, and may charge interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

## 5. Timeline and acceptance

5.1 NunyaLink will use reasonable efforts to meet the timeline in the proposal. Timings are estimates unless agreed in writing as fixed dates.

5.2 When a Deliverable is ready, the Client will have **5 working days** to test it against the Specification and report any failures in writing. NunyaLink will fix reported failures and resubmit.

5.3 A Deliverable is accepted when the Client confirms acceptance, starts using it in live operation, or does not report a failure within the testing period.

## 6. Support and warranty

6.1 For **[30] days** after acceptance, NunyaLink will fix, at no extra cost, any failure of a Deliverable to work in line with the Specification.

6.2 This does not cover problems caused by changes made by anyone other than NunyaLink, misuse, or changes to third-party services outside NunyaLink's control. Ongoing maintenance and new work can be provided under a separate support arrangement.

## 7. Intellectual property

7.1 On full payment of the fees, the Client will own the intellectual property rights in the bespoke Deliverables created specifically for it.

7.2 NunyaLink keeps ownership of its pre-existing materials, know-how, templates, code libraries and tools ("**Background Materials**"). Where Background Materials form part of a Deliverable, NunyaLink grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual licence to use them as part of that Deliverable for its own business.

7.3 Third-party software and services remain subject to their own licence terms.

7.4 Unless the Client objects in writing, NunyaLink may describe the project in general terms in its portfolio, without disclosing confidential information.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's confidential information secret and use it only for this agreement. This does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the receiving party, was already lawfully known to it, or must be disclosed by law. This clause continues after the agreement ends.

## 9. Data protection

9.1 Each party will comply with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

9.2 Where NunyaLink processes personal data on the Client's behalf, it acts as the Client's processor and will:

- process the data only on the Client's documented instructions;
- make sure anyone handling the data is bound by confidentiality;
- keep the data secure using appropriate technical and organisational measures;
- use sub-processors only with the Client's general authorisation, and tell the Client of any changes;
- help the Client respond to data subject requests and meet its security obligations;
- tell the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach; and
- delete or return the data at the end of the Services, unless the law requires otherwise.

9.3 The parties will sign a separate data processing agreement where the nature of the processing requires one.

## 10. Liability

10.1 Nothing in this agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or anything else that cannot be limited by law.

10.2 Neither party is liable for any loss of profits, revenue, business, goodwill or data, or for any indirect or consequential loss.

10.3 Subject to 10.1, NunyaLink's total liability under or in connection with this agreement is limited to the total fees paid by the Client under it.

## 11. Term and termination

11.1 This agreement starts on the date it is signed and continues until the Services are complete, unless ended earlier under this clause.

11.2 Either party may end this agreement by giving **30 days'** written notice.

11.3 Either party may end this agreement immediately by written notice if the other materially breaches it and does not put the breach right within 14 days of being asked to, or becomes insolvent.

11.4 If the agreement ends, the Client will pay for all work done and costs committed up to the end date. Any deposit covers work already carried out and is non-refundable to that extent.

## 12. General

12.1 Neither party is liable for delay caused by events outside its reasonable control.

12.2 NunyaLink may use subcontractors but remains responsible for their work.

12.3 Neither party may transfer this agreement without the other's written consent.

12.4 This agreement, the proposal and Schedule 1 are the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter. If they conflict, this agreement takes priority.

12.5 Changes to this agreement must be in writing and agreed by both parties.

12.6 No one other than the parties has any right to enforce this agreement under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.

12.7 This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
